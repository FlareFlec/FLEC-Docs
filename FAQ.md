# FAQ

## 1. What is FLEC?

FLEC (Flare • LATAM • Evolution • Community) is a community-driven initiative built within the Flare ecosystem, focused on participation, education, digital collectibles, and community growth.

---

## 2. What does FLEC stand for?

FLEC stands for Flare, LATAM, Evolution, and Community, representing the core pillars of the ecosystem.

---

## 3. Why was FLEC created?

FLEC was created to bring together community members, encourage participation, and explore new digital experiences within the Flare ecosystem.

---

## 4. Is FLEC an official Flare project?

No. FLEC is an independent community initiative built within the Flare ecosystem and inspired by its technology and community values.

---

## 5. How can I participate in FLEC?

Community members can participate through events, educational initiatives, community activities, future digital collectibles, and ecosystem engagement opportunities as they become available.

---

## 6. What is the total supply of FLEC?

The total supply is 100,000,000 FLEC.

---

## 7. How is the token supply distributed?

The token allocation is described in the Tokenomics section of the documentation.

---

## 8. What are Genesis Pods?

Genesis Pods are the first planned digital collectible collection within the FLEC ecosystem and serve as the foundation for future collectible experiences.

---

## 9. What is the Genesis Pod Reveal?

The Genesis Pod Reveal is the process through which the contents or characteristics of Genesis Pods become visible after distribution.

---

## 10. Will Genesis Pods evolve?

The ecosystem plans to explore an Evolution Framework that may allow future collectible progression and interactive experiences.

---

## 11. What is the Evolution Framework?

The Evolution Framework is a planned system designed to support future collectible growth, progression, and community participation.

---

## 12. Will NFTs have utility?

FLEC plans to explore future NFT utilities as the ecosystem develops. Specific features may evolve over time.

---

## 13. Will there be community governance?

The roadmap includes governance exploration as a future initiative. Details may develop as the ecosystem grows.

---

## 14. Where can I find official FLEC links?

Official FLEC resources, social channels, and community links can be found through the official FLEC Linktree:

https://linktr.ee/flareflec

---

## 15. Is participation in FLEC guaranteed to generate profits?

No. FLEC is a community initiative. Nothing within the ecosystem should be interpreted as financial, investment, or legal advice, and participation involves risk.
