# FLEC FAQ

## English

### 1. What is FLEC?

FLEC stands for **Flare, LATAM, Evolution, and Community**.

FLEC is a community-driven initiative created to help Spanish-speaking users across Latin America discover Flare, understand its ecosystem, and participate with greater knowledge, safety, and confidence.

Its long-term vision brings together education, community onboarding, ecosystem discovery, the FLEC community token, and future digital experiences.

---

### 2. Why was FLEC created?

FLEC was created to help reduce the barriers that many Spanish-speaking users face when learning about and participating in blockchain ecosystems.

These barriers may include limited beginner-friendly education, language gaps, fragmented information, insufficient wallet-safety guidance, and unclear pathways for discovering ecosystem applications.

FLEC seeks to provide accessible education, safer onboarding, community support, and clearer connections between Spanish-speaking users and the Flare ecosystem.

---

### 3. What is FLEC Hub?

FLEC Hub is intended to become the central educational and community access point of the FLEC ecosystem.

Its long-term purpose is to provide Spanish-first learning experiences related to Flare, wallet safety, ecosystem discovery, and responsible participation.

Future versions may include progressive learning modules, educational pathways, project tutorials, community workshops, learning-progress tools, and collaborations with teams across the Flare ecosystem.

---

### 4. Is FLEC Hub already fully operational?

No.

FLEC Hub is under active development. Some educational and onboarding components may be introduced progressively, while other features remain part of the long-term roadmap.

No feature should be considered active, guaranteed, or commercially available unless it has been formally announced through official FLEC channels.

---

### 5. What is the FLEC token?

FLEC is a community token deployed on the Flare network.

Contract address:

`0xb71E9a49a3151484753eacF9513b6876C6BEa62b`

The token forms part of the broader FLEC ecosystem. Its future role may include supporting educational initiatives, community programs, ecosystem campaigns, selected digital experiences, and long-term ecosystem development.

Any future utility will remain subject to technical development, security review, legal considerations, and formal approval.

---

### 6. Does FLEC promise rewards or returns?

No.

FLEC does not guarantee financial returns, token appreciation, NFT value, rewards, access, partnerships, or future benefits.

Any future community reward, token utility, campaign, or digital experience will require appropriate review before implementation.

FLEC is focused on education, community participation, safer onboarding, and long-term ecosystem development.

---

### 7. What is Genesis Pod / OVO?

The Colibri Genesis journey begins with the **Genesis Pod**, represented in its first stage as **OVO**.

OVO symbolizes the beginning of the user’s journey within the FLEC ecosystem. It is designed as a digital collectible that may evolve as the user learns, participates, and develops a stronger connection with the community.

Genesis Pod and OVO are not separate collectibles. OVO is the first planned stage of the Genesis Pod.

---

### 8. What is the NFT evolution path?

The planned NFT evolution path is:

**OVO → Nest → Feathers → Flight → Guardian**

Each stage is intended to represent growth, identity, participation, and long-term connection with FLEC.

Any future NFT sale, evolution mechanism, utility, or reward will require technical, security, operational, and legal review before implementation.

---

### 9. Will FLEC use FCC?

FLEC may explore future uses of **Flare Confidential Compute (FCC)** within FLEC Hub.

Potential areas may include privacy, eligibility verification, fraud prevention, and other security-sensitive processes.

No FCC integration should be considered active or guaranteed. Any implementation would require technical validation, security review, and alignment with Flare’s infrastructure and standards.

---

### 10. Will FLEC operate RPC infrastructure?

FLEC may explore community-focused RPC infrastructure in the future.

The objective would be to improve regional access, education, testing, and support for users and builders across Latin America.

This remains a long-term objective and will depend on technical capacity, operational sustainability, security, and ecosystem requirements.

---

### 11. Will FLEC become an FTSO provider?

FLEC may evaluate future participation in the **Flare Time Series Oracle (FTSO)**.

The long-term ambition is to contribute to one of the earliest community-led FTSO initiatives operating from LATAM, if technically, operationally, economically, and legally viable.

FTSO participation is not currently active or guaranteed.

---

### 12. Is FLEC affiliated with Flare?

FLEC is an independent community-driven initiative built around the Flare ecosystem.

It should not be interpreted as an official product, subsidiary, or guaranteed partner of Flare Networks unless a formal relationship is publicly announced by the relevant parties.

FLEC is guided by FlareColombia and created by CryptoView.

---

### 13. How can users stay safe?

Users should:

- verify official FLEC links;
- carefully review wallet transactions and approvals;
- confirm networks and contract addresses;
- avoid suspicious websites and unsolicited messages;
- never share private keys or recovery phrases;
- conduct their own research;
- treat unannounced rewards, sales, or partnerships with caution.

FLEC will never request a user’s private keys or recovery phrase.
