---

## Community Distribution (30%)

Allocated to community initiatives, ecosystem participation, events, future rewards, and activities that support the growth and engagement of the FLEC community.

---

## Founders, Early Supporters & Early Builders (30%)

Allocated to the individuals and contributors who helped create, support, and build the foundations of the FLEC ecosystem from its earliest stages.

---

## Initial Liquidity (20%)

Allocated to support the initial liquidity of the FLEC token and help facilitate market accessibility within the ecosystem.

---

## Reserve & Future Ecosystem Development (20%)

Reserved to support the long-term sustainability, development, expansion, and future initiatives of the FLEC ecosystem. As the ecosystem evolves, future governance mechanisms and community participation may help guide decisions regarding the use of these funds.

---

## Disclaimer

Token allocations reflect the current vision of the FLEC ecosystem and may evolve as the project develops. Nothing contained in this documentation should be interpreted as financial, investment, or legal advice.
