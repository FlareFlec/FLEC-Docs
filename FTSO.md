# FLEC FTSO Data-Provider Development

FLEC Hub demonstrated FLEC's education and onboarding layer during Flare Summer Signal 2026.

The next active infrastructure workstream expands FLEC toward direct participation in Flare infrastructure. FLEC is beginning development toward FTSO data-provider capabilities as part of a future FLEC-operated Flare Entity, using Coston2 as the initial environment for development and operational validation.

This is a public development document. It records current work, staged objectives and the evidence required before any milestone is described as complete.

## Current Status

**Status:** ACTIVE — Research, Architecture & Coston2 Preparation

**Initial Network:** Flare Testnet Coston2

**Production Mainnet Status:** NOT DEPLOYED

Work has begun at the architecture and requirements stage. Future milestones will move progressively into testnet configuration, implementation, monitoring and operational validation. Those later stages are not yet complete.

## Why This Infrastructure Workstream Exists

FLEC began by building a Spanish-first education and onboarding product for Spanish-speaking users worldwide. The infrastructure workstream expands that mission through a longer development path:

**Education → Onboarding → Participation → Community → Infrastructure**

The long-term objective is for FLEC not only to teach users about Flare infrastructure, but also to develop the operational capabilities required to participate responsibly in that infrastructure. Latin America and Colombia remain important community roots and may provide a regional operating base, while the product and public educational mission serve Spanish-speaking users globally.

This workstream does not make claims about guaranteed rewards, profitability, yield or token price.

## FTSO and Flare Entity

FTSO data-provider capability is one part of the broader Flare Entity architecture. FLEC's immediate infrastructure focus is FTSO data-provider development.

A future complete FLEC-operated Flare Entity may involve additional infrastructure and protocol responsibilities. FTSO and Flare Entity therefore describe related but non-interchangeable scopes: the current active workstream develops one capability within a broader future architecture.

No production FTSO operation, deployed Flare Entity or registered validator is currently claimed.

## Coston2 First

Coston2 is the initial development and validation environment. The active development program will progressively cover:

- architecture;
- infrastructure requirements;
- FTSO data-provider requirements;
- validator/entity requirements;
- test configuration;
- monitoring;
- uptime;
- observability;
- operational security;
- automation;
- logging and alerts;
- failure recovery;
- data workflows;
- documentation;
- reproducible operational procedures.

An item will only be marked COMPLETE after supporting evidence exists.

## Development Stages

### Stage 1 — Architecture & Requirements

**ACTIVE**

Research and document official technical, operational and security requirements.

### Stage 2 — Coston2 Infrastructure Environment

**NEXT**

Prepare the initial test environment and configuration required for infrastructure validation.

### Stage 3 — Coston2 FTSO Data-Provider Validation

**NEXT**

Begin testnet implementation and validate data-provider workflows, monitoring, reliability and operational procedures.

### Stage 4 — Extended Test Operations

**PLANNED**

Operate the test environment for an extended period and collect evidence about reliability and operational behavior.

### Stage 5 — Production Readiness Review

**FUTURE**

Evaluate technical, security, operational, economic and legal readiness before any Mainnet decision.

### Stage 6 — Mainnet

**CONDITIONAL FUTURE**

Proceed only after successful validation and an explicit production-readiness decision.

FTSO data-provider development starts now. Mainnet operation does not start now, and no production launch date is promised.
